## SECTION 2 — ELIGIBILITY AND SCOPE

### 2.1 Eligibility

The Service is available to:

(a) Individual Indian nationals who are the registered owners of open plots located within the Company's current service areas;

(b) Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) who own open plots in the service area and hold valid OCI/PIO cards or Indian passports;

(c) Legal entities (companies, partnerships, trusts, HUFs) that are registered owners of open plots, subject to submission of appropriate authorisation documents.

### 2.2 Property Eligibility

The Service applies exclusively to:

(a) Open, vacant, or undeveloped plots of land;

(b) Plots with no active construction activity at the time of subscription;

(c) Plots that are physically accessible (not entirely surrounded by locked private property blocking all access);

(d) Plots located within the Company's current operational service zones (as updated from time to time on the Company's website).

**Properties NOT covered:**
- Plots with active legal disputes or court-imposed stay orders affecting the property
- Plots that are underwater, marshy, or otherwise permanently inaccessible
- Plots located in areas designated as restricted or sensitive zones by government authorities
- Agricultural land plots in remote areas outside the Company's service radius

### 2.3 Plot Size

- **Standard Plan** applies to plots up to and including 500 square yards (approximately 418 sq. metres).
- **Large Plot Plan** applies to plots exceeding 500 square yards.
- Plot size shall be as evidenced by the Subscriber's registered Sale Deed or official layout plan.
- In case of discrepancy between declared and actual plot size, the Company reserves the right to reclassify the plot to the appropriate plan and charge accordingly from the next billing cycle.

## SECTION 4 — SERVICE DELIVERY STANDARDS

### 4.1 Visit Frequency and Scheduling

(a) Two (2) monitoring visits shall be conducted per calendar month, approximately 15 days apart.

(b) Visits shall be conducted during daylight hours (typically 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).

(c) Advance notice of visits is not guaranteed and visits may be unannounced. Subscribers who wish to be informed before each visit must set this preference in their portal account (where this feature is available).

(d) The Company shall make its best efforts to complete both visits within each calendar month. If a visit is missed due to reasons attributable to the Company (other than force majeure), the Company shall conduct a compensatory visit in the following billing cycle.

(e) Missed visits due to plot inaccessibility caused by the Subscriber (locked, gated, or access denied) shall not entitle the Subscriber to a refund or compensatory visit.

### 4.2 Photography Standards

(a) Each visit shall produce a minimum of **eight (8) photographs** covering all four sides of the plot.

(b) Photographs will be taken using smartphones or digital cameras capable of capturing the plot and its surroundings adequately.

(c) Photographs will be time-stamped and, where technically possible, geo-tagged.

(d) The Company shall not use flash photography or drones without prior consent.

(e) The Company does not guarantee specific photograph angles, resolution, or quality beyond reasonable professional standards.

(f) Photographs will be uploaded to the portal within **48 working hours** of the visit.

### 4.3 Portal and Account Standards

(a) Portal uptime shall be maintained at a best-effort basis. Scheduled maintenance windows shall be notified to subscribers in advance.

(b) The Company shall maintain photograph archives for the duration of the active subscription plus a 30-day retrieval window post-termination.

(c) Portal access is provided on an "as-is" basis. The Company is not liable for data loss due to force majeure or third-party technical failures beyond its control.

(d) Each subscriber account is for one (1) plot. Additional plots require separate subscriptions.

(e) The Subscriber is responsible for maintaining the security of portal login credentials.

### 4.4 Alert Notifications

(a) Automated email and/or SMS alerts shall be sent to the Subscriber's registered contact details upon every photograph upload.

(b) The Subscriber must ensure their registered email and mobile number are current and active.

(c) The Company is not liable for failed notifications due to the Subscriber's inactive email/phone or spam filters.

## SECTION 6 — WHAT THE SERVICE IS AND IS NOT

### 6.1 Nature of Service — Important Clarifications

The Subscriber expressly acknowledges and understands that:

**(a) Monitoring, NOT Security:**
The Service provides visual monitoring through periodic visits and photographs. It is **not** a security service. The Company does not deploy guards, install cameras, or provide continuous surveillance.

**(b) Photography, NOT Certification:**
Photographs are for the Subscriber's personal monitoring use. They do **not** constitute legal evidence, survey reports, or certificates for use in court proceedings, property transactions, or regulatory filings.

**(c) Observation Reporting, NOT Legal Action:**
If the field executive observes encroachment or anomalies, this will be noted in the visit report. The Company will **not** take any legal action, file complaints, or intervene on the Subscriber's behalf. All legal remedies remain solely the Subscriber's responsibility.

**(d) Management, NOT Maintenance:**
"Property Management" in the context of this Service refers to remote monitoring and documentation only. It **does not** include physical maintenance such as clearing vegetation, cleaning, fencing, or any civil work.

### 6.2 What is Explicitly Excluded

The following activities are outside the scope of this Service and shall NOT be provided under the subscription:

- Physical clearance of weeds, shrubs, or vegetation
- Debris removal or waste disposal
- Fencing, gate, or boundary wall installation or repair
- Security guard deployment
- Legal notices or court proceedings against encroachers
- Liaison with municipal, revenue, or government authorities
- Payment of property taxes or utility bills
- Preparation of legal documents or affidavits
- Title search or advocate opinion
- Survey, re-measurement, or demarcation by a licensed surveyor
- Any activity requiring government licensing or specialised certification
