## PART III — BOUNDARY DEMARCATION (Schedule C)

### 3.1 Owner's Obligation to Demarcate

The Subscriber hereby agrees and undertakes that before the first scheduled visit by the Company's representative, the Subscriber shall:

(a) Personally be present at the plot, OR arrange for a duly authorised representative to be present, to physically show and demarcate the exact boundaries of the plot to the Company's representative;

(b) Point out all four corner boundary pillars, boundary stones, compound wall edges, or natural markers that define the exact extent of the plot;

(c) Ensure that the plot boundaries are clearly identifiable and, where not, take steps to place boundary markers (stones/pillars) at the four corners at the Subscriber's own cost and before the first service visit;

(d) Provide a sketch or hand-drawn boundary map if formal survey maps are not available.

### 3.2 Consequence of Non-Demarcation

Failure by the Subscriber to demarcate boundaries before the first scheduled visit shall entitle the Company to defer commencement of services until such demarcation is completed. The subscription period shall commence only from the date of successful boundary demarcation and first visit, notwithstanding payment.

### 3.3 Liability for Incorrect Boundaries

The Company's monitoring services shall be limited strictly to the boundaries as demarcated and shown by the Subscriber. The Company shall bear no liability for any encroachment, trespass, or boundary dispute that arises due to incorrect demarcation by the Subscriber.

## PART IV — SERVICES PROVIDED

### 4.1 Standard Services (Included in Subscription)

The Company agrees to provide the following services to the Subscriber during the term of this Agreement:

**A. Physical Site Visits**

- Two (2) physical visits per calendar month to the Subscriber's plot.
- Visits shall be conducted by an authorised representative or field executive of the Company.
- Visit frequency: approximately once every 15 days (fortnightly).
- The Company shall endeavour to conduct visits as scheduled; however, visits may be rescheduled due to force majeure events, inclement weather, public holidays, or operational exigencies, with rescheduling within the same calendar month.

**B. Photographic Documentation**

- During each visit, the Company's representative shall capture photographs of the plot from all four cardinal directions (North, South, East, West), plus any specific angles or areas of interest.
- A minimum of eight (8) photographs shall be captured per visit.
- Photographs shall be geo-tagged (GPS-stamped) and time-stamped where technically possible.
- All photographs shall be uploaded to the Subscriber's online account portal within 48 working hours of each visit.
- Photos shall be uploaded every fifteen (15) days, corresponding to each fortnightly visit.

**C. Online Account & Digital Access**

- The Company shall create and maintain a dedicated online subscriber account for the Subscriber on the Company's platform/portal.
- The Subscriber shall receive login credentials (username and password) upon account activation.
- The Subscriber may access the account from any device via web browser or mobile application (as available).
- The Subscriber shall receive automated email and/or SMS alert notifications each time new photographs are uploaded to their account.
- Account history shall be maintained for the duration of the active subscription.

**D. Preliminary Observation Reporting**

- The field representative shall note and report to the Company any obvious observations during visits, including:
  - Visible encroachment or boundary markers displaced
  - Debris dumping or solid waste accumulation
  - Vegetation overgrowth obscuring boundaries
  - Any visible signage, construction activity, or third-party presence
- A brief visit summary note shall accompany the photograph upload on the portal.

### 4.2 Services NOT Included (Out of Scope)

The following services are expressly NOT included in the subscription unless separately agreed upon in writing and at additional cost:

| Out-of-Scope Service | Reason |
|---|---|
| Legal action against encroachments | Requires separate legal engagement |
| Land demarcation / re-survey | Requires licensed surveyor |
| Fencing, boundary wall construction | Civil work; separate contract required |
| Weed/vegetation clearance | Maintenance work; separate quote required |
| Property tax payments | Owner's responsibility |
| Insurance procurement | Owner's responsibility |
| Title verification / legal opinion | Requires qualified advocate |
| Utility bill payments | Owner's responsibility |

| Sale/purchase facilitation | Separate brokerage agreement required |
| Security guard deployment | Separate arrangement; additional cost |
| GHMC/HMDA/DTCP liaison | Separate service; additional cost |

### 4.3 Future Services (Proposed)

The Company intends to expand its property management services to include built-up structures, residential and commercial properties in future. Subscribers will be notified of new service offerings as they become available.

## PART VI — ONBOARDING PROCESS

### 6.1 Step-by-Step Onboarding

**Step 1 — Enquiry and Plan Selection**
- Subscriber contacts the Company via phone, email, or website inquiry form.
- Company executive explains service plans, pricing, and requirements.
- Subscriber selects appropriate plan based on plot size.

**Step 2 — Document Submission**
- Subscriber submits all mandatory documents listed in Schedule B (physical copies or clear scanned copies via email/portal).
- Company acknowledges receipt within 2 working days.

**Step 3 — Document Verification**
- Company reviews submitted documents for prima facie ownership confirmation.
- Company may request additional documents or clarification.
- Verification process: 3–5 working days.
- Subscriber is notified of verification outcome.

**Step 4 — Agreement Execution**
- This Agreement is prepared in duplicate.
- Both parties sign the Agreement on each page.
- Agreement is executed on non-judicial stamp paper of appropriate value (as per Telangana Stamp Duty rules — currently ₹100).
- One signed original retained by the Company; one signed original provided to the Subscriber.

**Step 5 — Payment**
- Subscriber makes the first month's payment (or 3-month advance payment).
- Company issues a GST Tax Invoice and payment receipt.

**Step 6 — Boundary Demarcation Visit (Mandatory)**
- Company schedules a dedicated boundary demarcation and onboarding site visit.
- The Subscriber (or authorised representative) MUST be present at this visit.
- The field executive photographs and records the exact boundary markers.
- GPS coordinates of boundary corners are recorded where possible.
- Onboarding site visit is not counted as one of the fortnightly monitoring visits.

**Step 7 — Account Activation**
- Company creates the Subscriber's online portal account.
- Login credentials (username + temporary password) are sent to the Subscriber's registered email.
- Subscriber is guided to change password on first login.

**Step 8 — Service Commencement**
- Regular fortnightly monitoring visits begin from the date following successful onboarding.
- Subscriber receives SMS/email alert when first photographs are uploaded.

## PART IX — TERM AND TERMINATION

### 9.1 Term of Agreement

This Agreement shall remain in force from the date of execution and shall continue for the minimum lock-in period of three (3) months, and thereafter on a month-to-month rolling basis unless terminated by either party as per this clause.

### 9.2 Termination by the Subscriber

(a) **During Lock-in Period:** The Subscriber may not terminate this Agreement during the initial three-month lock-in period. In exceptional circumstances (proven sale of the plot, death of the Subscriber, or documented force majeure), the Subscriber may submit a written request for early termination. The Company shall, at its sole discretion, consider refund of unused months after deducting a processing fee of ₹ 500 + GST.

(b) **After Lock-in Period:** The Subscriber may terminate this Agreement by providing **30 days' written notice** (email to the Company's registered email ID is accepted). No fees shall be refunded for the notice period month.

### 9.3 Termination by the Company

The Company may terminate this Agreement:

(a) **For Non-Payment:** If the Subscriber fails to pay dues within 30 days of suspension notice, with no further liability to refund any prepaid amounts for the non-served period exceeding 30 days.

(b) **For Material Breach:** Immediately, without notice, if the Subscriber has submitted forged/fraudulent documents, misrepresented ownership, or has used the service for any unlawful purpose.

(c) **For Convenience:** By providing 30 days' written notice to the Subscriber. In such case, the Company shall refund pro-rata fees for the unused period of any advance payment.

(d) **Force Majeure:** If performance is rendered impossible due to natural disasters, civil unrest, government orders, or similar events, after making reasonable attempts to resume service.

### 9.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:
- The Company shall disable the Subscriber's online portal access.
- The Company shall not be obliged to retain or provide portal photographs beyond 30 days of termination. The Subscriber may download all photographs from the portal within 30 days of termination notice.
- All original documents submitted by the Subscriber shall be returned (or confirmed destroyed if scanned copies only).
- Outstanding dues, if any, shall remain payable by the Subscriber.

## PART X — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

### 10.1 Limitation of Liability

The Company's services are limited to monitoring, photography, and reporting only. The Company shall not be liable for:

(a) Any encroachment, trespass, or unauthorised occupation of the plot that occurs or is ongoing;

(b) Any boundary disputes, title defects, or legal complications relating to the plot;

(c) Any damage, destruction, or loss to the plot or its surroundings;

(d) Any inability to conduct a visit due to inaccessibility, force majeure, or circumstances beyond the Company's control;

(e) Any inaccuracy in GPS coordinates or photograph geo-tags due to device limitations;

(f) Any loss of data on the portal due to technical failures, subject to the Company making reasonable backup provisions.

### 10.2 Maximum Liability

Notwithstanding any other provision, the Company's total aggregate liability to the Subscriber under this Agreement shall not exceed the total subscription fees paid by the Subscriber for the preceding three (3) months.

### 10.3 Indemnification by Subscriber

The Subscriber shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company, its officers, employees, agents, and representatives from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or related to:
