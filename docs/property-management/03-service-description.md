## ABOUT THIS SERVICE

REMAX Westside Realty's **Open Plot Property Management Service** gives plot owners peace of mind by regularly monitoring their land and providing photographic evidence of its condition — directly accessible online, from anywhere in the world.

This service is designed for:
- **NRI plot owners** who cannot visit their Hyderabad plots frequently
- **Working professionals** who own investment plots they rarely visit
- **Investors** with multiple plots who want systematic monitoring
- **Outstation owners** who purchased plots in Hyderabad from other cities
- **Senior citizens** who find it difficult to visit their plots regularly

### 1.2 The Two Fortnightly Visits — What Happens

Each monitoring visit consists of the following activities:

**Pre-Visit:**
- Field executive is assigned the visit in the Company's scheduling system
- Subscriber's boundary demarcation records are reviewed before the visit

**At the Plot:**

| Activity | Details |
|---|---|
| Boundary check | Field executive confirms that boundary markers and corners are intact and undisturbed |
| North-facing photograph | Photograph of the plot from the north side |
| South-facing photograph | Photograph of the plot from the south side |
| East-facing photograph | Photograph of the plot from the east side |
| West-facing photograph | Photograph of the plot from the west side |
| Adjacent road/approach | Photograph of the access road to the plot |
| Boundary marker photos | Close-up photos of boundary corner stones/markers |

| Observation notes | Field executive notes any visible anomalies (encroachment, dumping, construction nearby, vegetation growth, etc.) |

**Post-Visit:**
- Time-stamped, geo-tagged photographs are compiled
- Visit report (brief notes) is prepared
- All content is uploaded to the Subscriber's portal within 48 working hours
- Automated alert sent to Subscriber's registered email and mobile

### 2.2 Detailed Process Description

---

#### STEP 1 — ENQUIRY AND PLAN SELECTION

**Who does what:**

**Subscriber needs to provide (at this stage):**

- Plot size (approximate square yards)
- Ownership type (individual / NRI / joint / company)
- Preferred contact channel

---

#### STEP 2 — DOCUMENT SUBMISSION

**Who does what:**
- Subscriber compiles and submits the mandatory documents listed below.
- Documents may be submitted as:
  - Clear scanned PDFs / JPEG photographs via email
  - Physical photocopies at the Company's office
  - Via the onboarding portal link (where available)

**Mandatory Documents:**

| # | Document | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registered Sale Deed (copy) | Proof of purchase and ownership |
| 2 | Mother Deed / Prior sale deeds | Establishes title chain |
| 3 | Encumbrance Certificate (within 12 months) | Confirms no active loans/mortgages |
| 4 | Latest Property Tax Receipt | Confirms ownership and tax compliance |
| 5 | DTCP / HMDA approved layout plan | Confirms plot demarcation in approved layout |
| 6 | Pahani / Patta / RoR records | Revenue records confirming ownership |
| 7 | Aadhaar Card (self-attested copy) | KYC verification |
| 8 | PAN Card (self-attested copy) | KYC and GST compliance |
| 9 | 2 passport-size photographs | Account records |
| 10 | Address proof (utility bill etc.) | Correspondence address confirmation |

**Timeline for submission:** Subscriber's discretion; verification starts only after all documents received.

---

#### STEP 3 — DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

**Who does what:**
- Company's team reviews all submitted documents.
- Physical documents may be called for inspection if scanned copies are unclear.
- Company checks:
  - Sale Deed registered in Subscriber's name
  - Encumbrance Certificate shows no active encumbrances
  - Identity documents match the name on the Sale Deed
  - Plot location falls within the Company's service zone
- Company may request clarifications or additional documents.
- Subscriber is notified of:
  - **Successful verification** — proceed to Agreement signing, OR
  - **Pending requirements** — specific documents/clarifications needed, OR
  - **Rejection** — with reasons (e.g., plot outside service area, ownership unclear)

**Timeline:** 3–5 working days from receipt of complete documents

**Important:** The Company's verification is a good-faith visual review for KYC purposes only. It is NOT a legal title certification.

#### STEP 7 — BOUNDARY DEMARCATION VISIT (ONE-TIME, MANDATORY)

**Who does what:**
- Company schedules the boundary demarcation visit.
- **The Subscriber (or authorised representative) MUST be present at the plot.**
- Field executive meets the Subscriber at the plot.
- Subscriber physically walks the field executive along all four boundaries.
- Subscriber points out and confirms all four corner boundary markers.
- Field executive:
  - Photographs all four boundary corners
  - Takes an overview photograph of the full plot from an elevated vantage point
  - Records GPS coordinates at all four corners (where technically feasible)
  - Documents the access route to the plot
  - Notes any adjacent properties, roads, or landmarks as reference points
- Field executive and Subscriber co-sign the **Boundary Demarcation Record** (a one-page form confirming the recorded boundaries).
- Boundary Demarcation Record is uploaded to the Subscriber's portal.

**This visit is NOT counted as one of the fortnightly monitoring visits.**

**What if the Subscriber cannot be present?**
- Subscriber may authorise a representative in writing.
- Representative must hold a notarised Power of Attorney or written authorisation letter.
- Authorised representative must have personal knowledge of the exact plot boundaries.
- If no authorised person is available, the visit is rescheduled. Service commencement is delayed until demarcation is complete.

**Timeline:** Scheduled within 7 days of account activation

---

#### STEP 8 — SERVICE ACTIVE — FORTNIGHTLY MONITORING VISITS

**Who does what:**
- From the day after successful boundary demarcation, the plot enters the active monitoring cycle.
- First fortnightly visit scheduled within 15 days of demarcation.
- Second visit of the month scheduled approximately 15 days after the first.
- This cycle repeats every month throughout the subscription.

**What the field executive does at each visit:**
1. Cross-references Subscriber's boundary demarcation record before the visit.
2. Visits the plot during daylight hours.
3. Conducts a full visual check of the plot boundary markers.
4. Captures minimum 8 photographs (4 directional, 2 boundary markers, 1 approach road, 1 overview).
5. Notes any observations (anomalies, encroachment signs, vegetation growth, waste dumping, nearby construction activity, etc.).
6. Records visit time, date, and GPS stamp.
7. Returns to office and uploads all photographs and notes within 48 working hours.

---

#### STEP 9 — PHOTOGRAPH UPLOAD AND SUBSCRIBER ALERT

**Who does what:**
- Company's field executive or back-office team uploads photographs to the portal.
- System auto-generates upload timestamp.
- Subscriber receives automatic email alert: *"New photographs have been uploaded to your property [Plot Reference]. Log in to view."*
- Subscriber receives SMS alert to registered mobile.
- Subscriber can log in at any time to view, browse, and download photographs.

---

#### STEP 10 — MONTHLY BILLING (RECURRING)

**Who does what:**
- Monthly invoice is generated on the billing anniversary date.
- Invoice is emailed to the Subscriber's registered email.
- Subscriber pays by the 1st of each month (5-day grace period applies).
- Company sends payment reminders if payment is delayed.
- Subscription continues uninterrupted upon payment receipt.
- GST Tax Invoice is issued for every payment.

### 2.3 Process for Reporting an Anomaly

If the field executive observes something unusual during a visit:

1. **Observation is documented** — photographs and written notes are taken.
2. **Report uploaded to portal** — with "Attention Required" flag where applicable.
3. **Subscriber is notified** — via email/SMS alert, in addition to the standard upload alert.
4. **Action is the Subscriber's responsibility** — the Company does NOT take independent action (no legal notices, no complaints filed, no physical intervention).
5. **Subscriber contacts the Company** for guidance on next steps (who to call, which authority to approach).
6. If encroachment is observed, the Company can provide a list of property lawyers or GHMC/revenue office contact points as a value-add.

## SECTION 5 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

**Q1. I live in the USA. Can I subscribe to this service for my Hyderabad plot?**
Yes. NRIs can subscribe. You will need to send document scans via email, sign the Agreement (or through a notarised PoA representative in India), and pay via international wire transfer or UPI (if your Indian bank is linked). You will be able to access your portal from anywhere in the world.

**Q2. Do I need to be present for every visit?**
No. You only need to be present (or send an authorised representative) for the one-time boundary demarcation visit at onboarding. All subsequent fortnightly monitoring visits are conducted by our field executive without the Subscriber present.

**Q3. What happens if our field executive finds an encroachment?**
We will photograph it, note it in the visit report, flag it prominently in the portal, and send you an alert. Taking legal or physical action is not within our scope — you will need to engage a property lawyer or contact the relevant revenue/police authorities. We can provide guidance and referrals if needed.

**Q4. My plot has no boundary markers. What should I do?**
You will need to place boundary corner stones or markers before the boundary demarcation visit. You can hire a local surveyor or mason for this. Our field executive cannot visit until clear boundaries are identifiable.

**Q5. Can I subscribe for more than one plot?**
Yes. Each plot requires a separate subscription and a separate Service Agreement. Discounts for multiple plots may be available — please enquire with our team.

**Q6. What if a visit is missed in a month?**
If a visit is missed due to reasons on our side (not force majeure), we will conduct a compensatory visit in the next billing cycle. Visits missed due to inaccessibility caused by the Subscriber will not be compensated.

**Q7. How long are my photos stored on the portal?**
Photos are stored for the duration of your active subscription plus 30 days post-termination. We strongly recommend downloading your photo archive before terminating the subscription.

**Q8. Can I cancel after the 3-month lock-in period?**
Yes. After the lock-in period, you may cancel with 30 days' written notice. No penalty applies.

**Q9. Is this service compliant with RERA?**
REMAX Westside Realty is a RERA-registered real estate agent. Property management monitoring services of this nature (visiting, photography, reporting) do not require additional RERA registration beyond agent registration.

**Q10. Will the Company take photographs inside my plot or just from the boundary?**
Our field executive will photograph the plot from outside its boundaries and from within the plot boundary (not inside any locked structure). All photographs are taken in open, accessible areas.
